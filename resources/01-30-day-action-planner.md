# LaunchPilot 30-Day Digital Business Action Planner

A planning tool, not a guarantee. Thirty days is enough to research, validate, and prepare a first offer. It is not enough to promise sales. Work at your own pace and adjust the plan to your time and budget.

## How to use this planner

1. Read the Week overview once, end to end.
2. At the start of each week, fill in that week's goal and milestone.
3. Each day, complete the tasks in the Daily checklist (15 to 60 minutes is realistic).
4. Every Sunday, complete the Weekly review. Then decide what to keep, change, or drop.
5. Keep a single copy of your notes. Do not start over if you miss a day. Pick up where you left off.

## Week overview

| Week | Theme | Milestone you should reach |
|------|-------|----------------------------|
| 1 (Days 1 to 7) | Discover and shortlist | Three shortlisted business ideas |
| 2 (Days 8 to 14) | Validate demand | Conversations with at least 5 potential customers and one direction chosen |
| 3 (Days 15 to 21) | Plan and build | A first offer, a draft landing page, and a working payment or waitlist test |
| 4 (Days 22 to 30) | Launch and review | Your offer shared with real people, with results recorded and reviewed |

## Week 1: Discover and shortlist (Days 1 to 7)

**Weekly goal:** ______________________________

**Daily tasks**

- Day 1: List 10 digital business ideas that come to mind, without judging them.
- Day 2: For each idea, write who the customer would be and what problem it solves.
- Day 3: Score each idea using the Niche Selection scorecard (see the Idea Library framework).
- Day 4: Research 3 existing competitors or alternatives for your top 5 ideas. Note their price and offer (do not copy them).
- Day 5: Check time and budget fit. Remove any idea you cannot start within your limits.
- Day 6: Pick your three shortlisted ideas.
- Day 7: Weekly review.

**Milestone check:** [ ] Three shortlisted ideas written down.

## Week 2: Validate demand (Days 8 to 14)

**Weekly goal:** ______________________________

**Daily tasks**

- Day 8: Write 5 open questions for potential customers about the problem (not about your solution).
- Day 9: Find 5 people who match your target customer. Send a short, polite message asking for a 15-minute conversation.
- Day 10: Hold 1 or 2 conversations. Record the exact words people use to describe their problem.
- Day 11: Hold more conversations. Note what they already pay for or use to solve the problem.
- Day 12: Review your notes. Look for repeated problems, not one-off comments.
- Day 13: Choose one direction. Write why it fits your skills and budget.
- Day 14: Weekly review.

**Good validation signs** (judge honestly; these are signals, not proof): people describe the problem in their own words without prompting, they already spend time or money on a solution, and they ask when they can get something.

**Milestone check:** [ ] At least 5 conversations recorded. [ ] One direction chosen.

## Week 3: Plan and build (Days 15 to 21)

**Weekly goal:** ______________________________

**Daily tasks**

- Day 15: Write your offer on one page: who it is for, the problem, what is included, how it is delivered, and the price you are testing.
- Day 16: List the deliverables exactly (for example, "a 20-page PDF with 5 worksheets"). Do not promise results you cannot control.
- Day 17: Write a draft landing page using the Landing Page Builder Prompt Kit.
- Day 18: Write 3 FAQs and 2 honest objection answers (cost, time, refunds, what is not included).
- Day 19: Set up a simple way to take a sign-up or payment. Test it with your own account first.
- Day 20: Create your first content piece (a post, short video, or carousel) about the problem.
- Day 21: Weekly review.

**Milestone check:** [ ] Offer written. [ ] Landing page draft done. [ ] Sign-up or payment tested with your own account.

## Week 4: Launch and review (Days 22 to 30)

**Weekly goal:** ______________________________

**Daily tasks**

- Day 22: Share your offer with your existing network and the people you interviewed, if they agreed.
- Day 23: Post your content piece. Reply to every comment and message.
- Day 24: Check that the landing page works on a phone and on a computer.
- Day 25: Check your delivery process. Confirm that a buyer or sign-up receives what was promised.
- Day 26: Record your numbers: visitors, sign-ups, buyers, refunds, and questions received.
- Day 27: Read every question you received. Update your FAQ.
- Day 28: Decide on one change to test next (one change at a time).
- Day 29: Plan your next 30 days.
- Day 30: Final review.

**Milestone check:** [ ] Offer shared. [ ] Numbers recorded. [ ] One improvement chosen.

## Weekly review (use every Sunday)

1. What did I finish this week?
2. What took longer than expected?
3. What did customers or people tell me?
4. What will I keep doing, change, or stop?
5. What is the one task for next week that matters most?

## Notes on legal, tax, and financial matters

This planner gives educational guidance only. It is not legal, tax, or financial advice. Check local rules on business registration, taxes, consumer protection, privacy, refunds, and platform terms with a qualified professional before you take payments or run ads.
