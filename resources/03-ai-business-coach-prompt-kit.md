# LaunchPilot AI Business Coach Prompt Kit

**Status: prompt-based alternative.** This kit is a set of prompts you paste into an AI assistant of your choice. It is not a live chatbot inside LaunchPilot. No automated service is running behind it.

It helps you shortlist digital business ideas based on your skills, interests, budget, and available time. It gives you options to test, not a guaranteed winner. No AI tool can tell you which business will succeed. Your own research with real customers is what tells you that.

## How to use this kit

1. Fill in the Profile Sheet below honestly. Vague answers produce vague results.
2. Paste the Master Prompt into your AI assistant, followed by your Profile Sheet.
3. Work through the prompts in order. Each builds on the last.
4. Verify every suggestion yourself before spending money or time on it.

## Master Prompt (paste this first)

```
You are a practical business-idea coach for a beginner. Your role is to help me explore digital business ideas. You do not promise income, guarantee success, or claim any idea is certain to be profitable.

Rules:
- Ask for missing information before recommending anything.
- Base suggestions only on the profile I give you.
- For each suggestion, state the main risk and one way to test demand cheaply.
- Do not invent market sizes, search volumes, profit margins, or success rates.
- Say clearly when you are not sure.
- Keep explanations plain and avoid jargon. If you use a term, explain it in one sentence.

Here is my profile:
[paste your Profile Sheet]
```

## Prompt 1: Summarise my profile

```
Summarise my profile in 5 bullet points. Then list up to 3 questions you need answered before suggesting ideas. Wait for my answers.
```

## Prompt 2: Generate candidate ideas

```
Suggest 10 digital business ideas that fit my profile. Spread them across different types (for example: digital product, freelance service, template, coaching, content, or AI-assisted service).

For each idea, give:
1. Idea name
2. Business model (one-time sale, subscription, service fee, or other)
3. Who the customer is
4. The problem it solves
5. Skills I already have that apply
6. Skills or tools I would need to learn
7. Rough startup cost category: low, medium, or high (do not give exact prices)
8. Main risk
```

## Prompt 3: Score the ideas (decision aid only)

```
Score each idea from 1 (poor fit) to 5 (strong fit) on:
- Fit with my skills
- Fit with my interests
- Fit with my weekly time
- Fit with my budget
- How easily I can reach customers
- How important the problem seems to be (I will verify this with real people)
- How easy it is to test demand quickly
- How crowded the competition may be
- How complex delivery would be
- Fit with the business model I prefer

Show the scores in a table, then the total. Explain that the score is a decision aid, not proof that the idea will work.
```

## Prompt 4: Shortlist three ideas

```
From the scored list, choose my top 3. For each one, explain:
- Why it fits my profile
- What would make it a poor choice for me
- The first test I could run in 7 days without spending more than my budget
```

## Prompt 5: Plan demand validation

```
For my top idea, write:
1. Five open-ended questions for a 15-minute customer conversation. Do not pitch the solution in the questions.
2. Three places to find people who match the customer (for example, communities, forums, or local groups). Explain how to join them respectfully.
3. Three signs that demand is real, and three signs that it is weak.
4. A simple way to record what customers say, using their own words.
```

## Prompt 6: Create a first-offer concept

```
Based on what I learn from conversations, help me draft a first offer:
- Who it is for
- The problem it solves
- What is included (as a list of concrete deliverables)
- How it is delivered
- Price options to test, with the reasoning for each
- What it does not include
```

## Prompt 7: Check my plan for honesty

```
Review my offer and marketing plan. Flag:
- Any income or results claims
- Any fake or unverifiable social proof
- Any artificial urgency
- Any missing refund, support, or delivery information
- Any claim I cannot back up with evidence
Suggest honest alternatives.
```

## Prompt 8: Build a 30-day plan

```
Turn my chosen idea into a 30-day plan using four weekly goals: discover, validate, build, and launch. Give daily tasks of 15 to 60 minutes each, matched to my weekly time budget. Mark which tasks need my own verification.
```

## Limits of this kit

- The assistant can be wrong. Check facts, prices, and platform rules yourself.
- It cannot know your local market, legal position, or tax situation.
- It does not replace customer interviews, small tests, or professional advice.
- Ideas are not ranked by how much money they make. Use the scores only to decide what to test next.
